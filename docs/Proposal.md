# s-CPD: Student Co-Curricular Points and Development System Proposal Report

## 1\. Introduction

The Student Co-Curricular Points and Development (s-CPD) system is a proposed Android application designed to make it easier to manage student activities, event bookings, attendance, and co-curricular points. The system has three types of users: students, club committee members, and s-CPD administrators. Students can browse approved events, book places, record attendance, and check their points history. Club committee members can create and manage events, request points for activities, and track participation, while administrators review and approve events and check attendance and points records.

To ensure that attendance is recorded correctly and points are awarded fairly, the system will use QR codes, biometric verification, and registered-device checks. It will also allow students to record attendance without an internet connection and upload the records once the connection is restored. Other features, such as different access permissions for each user role, booking restrictions, and a record of all points awarded or removed, will help keep the system secure and reliable.

This proposal describes the system's requirements, architecture, and design as a foundation for its future development and testing.

## 2\. Project Aims and Objectives

### 2.1 Aim

The aim of the s-CPD project is to develop an Android application that simplifies the management of student activities, bookings, attendance, and points while keeping records accurate and secure, even when the internet connection is unstable.

### 2.2 Objectives

The project aims to achieve the following objectives:

**1\. Activity and booking management**: Allow students to find approved events, book available places, avoid schedule clashes, and receive event notifications.

**2\. Event management and approval**: Allow club committee members to create and manage events and request points, while administrators approve or reject events.

**3\. Attendance verification**: Use QR codes, biometric verification, and registered devices to reduce fake attendance records and unauthorised check-ins.

**4\. Offline attendance**: Allow students to record attendance without internet access and upload their records when the connection returns.

**5\. Points management**: Ensure points are awarded only for verified attendance and keep a record of points awarded, changed, or removed, including the reasons for any changes.

**6\. User access and data security**: Give students, club committee members, and administrators different permissions to prevent unauthorised actions and changes to records.

**7\. Attendance monitoring and problem handling**: Show students the status of their attendance records, explain why records are rejected, and allow suspicious records or special cases to be reviewed.

## 4\. Conclusion

In conclusion, the proposed s-CPD system aims to provide a single platform for managing student activities, event bookings, attendance, and co-curricular points. Features such as event approval, QR-code check-ins, biometric verification, and registered-device checks are intended to reduce false attendance records and ensure that students receive points for activities they have actually attended.

The system will also support attendance recording without an internet connection and upload records when connectivity returns. Access permissions and a clear history of points changes will help protect records and ensure accountability. The requirements analysis, system architecture, and system design will guide the development and testing of the application, with the goal of making co-curricular management easier and more reliable for students, club committees, and s-CPD administrators.
